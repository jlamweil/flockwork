## The thread (finding 1 of the 09-19 addendum, now diagnosed)

`origin_body`/audit "needs the `ssh://example-host-a/home/you/...` URL form;
scp-form breaks origin-host reads with a confusing FileNotFoundError"
— while the addendum's own "How to claim" line documents the scp-form:
`SWARM_ORIGIN=you@example-host-a:solve-metrics-origin.git python3 l2/inrepo.py
worker <label>`. The lane's documented invocation ships a form that
breaks every origin-host body read.

**Causal argument.** The two transports of the lane diverge: git plumbing
(ls-remote/push/clone/fetch — seed, claim, return, sweep's ref moves)
parses URLs itself and natively accepts both `ssh://host/path` and
scp-form `user@host:path`; but `origin_raw()` (l2/inrepo.py, T5 law:
read bodies on the ORIGIN host) constructs an ssh COMMAND from the URL
and only recognizes the `ssh://` scheme. scp-form falls through to the
local-path branch, `git cat-file` runs with `cwd=<the literal URL
string>`, and subprocess raises FileNotFoundError — which `sweep()`'s
att-recovery and `reconcile()`'s dating inherit (reconcile does not even
catch it). One dispatch point, every origin-host read behind it.
